## Who is Homelab-Alpha?

Hey there!

Welcome to the **Homelab-Alpha**! I'm GJS, a tech enthusiast driven by a passion
for automation, infrastructure, and creative problem solving. My homelab is my
ultimate playground; a space where I experiment with emerging technologies,
refine configurations, and constantly hunt for ways to make my digital
environment smarter and more efficient.

I am a strong advocate for the open-source movement and the power of
self-hosting. My goal is to make technology more accessible and robust for
everyone. Whether I'm streamlining deployments, hardening environments, or
squeezing out every bit of performance, I am always in pursuit of innovative
solutions.

I thrive on tackling complex architectural challenges and automating the
mundane. From discovering the latest gadgets to deep diving into advanced
technical builds, I am at my best when I am tinkering under the hood.

My repositories focus on the pillars of modern self-hosting: automation,
security, and system optimization. Featuring everything from Docker Compose
stacks and OpenSSL certificates to AdGuard Home filter lists and shell scripts,
each project is built to empower your home lab. Explore my work, and feel free
to collaborate!

<br />

## Who is Homelab-Alpha for?

Homelab-Alpha is a project dedicated to creating and managing a home laboratory
environment for personal and educational purposes. It aims to provide a platform
for experimenting with various technologies and learning new skills in a
hands-on environment.

<br />

## My Open Source Repositories

- **[AdGuard Home]:** Filter lists for AdGuard Home that block ads, trackers,
  phishing, and fraudulent websites.
- **[Docker]:** Docker Compose stacks, network guides, and an image template for
  a self-hosted homelab.
- **[dotfiles]:** Dotfiles and a menu-driven installer to customize and
  configure a Linux shell environment.
- **[OpenSSL]:** Configuration files and helper scripts to generate self-signed
  certificates for a homelab and for testing. Not suitable for production use.
- **[Shell Script]:** A collection of shell scripts and shell functions for
  system automation and optimization.
- **[Docs]:** The source of this documentation site, built with Hugo.
